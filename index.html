On touch screens, use Navigate to orbit and two fingers to zoom or pan. Projects autosave in this browser; Save project downloads a portable backup.

Models use LEGO part dimensions in LDraw units. Automatic slopes fit 45°, 65°, and 75° families to local height drops (2, 5, and 8 plates minimum). The 75° catalog family is labeled 73° by LEGO. Steeper terrain keeps supporting bricks above the slope; no piece is stretched. This editor models exterior shapes; it does not validate physical stability or part/color availability.
